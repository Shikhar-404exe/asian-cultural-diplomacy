# Non-Technical Publisher & Administrator Guide
## Asian Cultural Diplomacy Platform

Welcome to your publishing platform! This guide is written in clear, simple language specifically for you. You do not need any coding or technical knowledge to manage your digital publication, publish essays, approve reader reflections, announce public meetups, or back up your work.

---

## 1. How to Start the Website on Your Laptop

1. Open your laptop terminal (PowerShell or Command Prompt).
2. Type the following command and press **Enter**:
   ```bash
   npm run start
   ```
3. Your website is now running! Leave this terminal window open in the background while you or your visitors use the website.

---

## 2. How to Access the Website from Your Phone or Tablet

Your website is configured to be accessible by any phone, tablet, or secondary laptop connected to your home or office Wi-Fi network.

1. Connect your phone to the **same Wi-Fi network** as your laptop.
2. Open Safari, Chrome, or any mobile browser on your phone.
3. In the address bar, type your laptop's local address:
   ```
   http://192.168.1.8:3000
   ```
4. You will see your full website, beautifully formatted for your phone screen! You can bookmark this page or add it to your phone's home screen.

*(Note: Your laptop must remain powered on and awake while accessing from your phone).*

---

## 3. How to Log In to Your Publisher Portal

1. On your laptop or phone, click the **Founder Portal** pill button in the top navigation bar, or go directly to:
   ```
   http://localhost:3000/admin/login
   ```

   - **Email**: `founder@asianculturaldiplomacy.org`
   - **Password**: `CulturalDiplomacy2026!`

4. You are now in your private command centre!

## 4. How to Write and Publish an Article

Publishing an essay is as easy as using a word processor:

1. Click **Write a Blog** from the left sidebar or the dashboard overview.
2. **Title**: Enter the title of your essay (e.g., *"The Architecture of Dialogue"*).
3. **Subtitle**: Enter a brief one-sentence summary to intrigue readers.
4. **Category**: Choose the topic (e.g., *Diplomatic Essays*, *Art & Heritage*, or *Regional Dialogues*).
5. **Cover Image**: You can click one of the quick preset buttons to use your sister's artwork:
   - Click **Sister's Moon Bridge**
   - Click **Sister's Courtyard Pavilion**
   - Click **Mount Fuji & Sakura**
   - Or paste an image link from your Media Library.
6. **Featured Perspective**: Check the box if you want this article highlighted on your homepage hero.
7. **Write Content**: Click inside the editor box and type naturally. Use the formatting toolbar above the box:
   - Click **B** for bold
   - Click **H2** or **H3** for section titles
   - Click **Quote** for callout quotations
   - Click the image icon to insert illustrations
8. **Save as Draft or Publish**:
   - Click **Save as Draft** if you are still working on it.
   - Click **Publish Live** whenever you are ready for the world to read it!

---

## 5. How to Review and Approve Reader Comments

To protect your community from spam and maintain respectful academic civility, all public comments start in your **Moderation Queue**.

1. Click **Manage Comments** in the sidebar.
2. If readers have left comments, an amber alert will show at the top of your dashboard.
3. For each comment, you will see the commenter's name, their comment text, the article title, and date.
4. Click **Approve (Publish)**: The comment instantly becomes visible to all public readers beneath that article.
5. Click **Reject** or the trash icon: The comment will not appear publicly.

---

## 6. How to Add an Upcoming Event

1. Click **Manage Events** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Add New Event**.
3. Fill in:
   - Event Title (e.g., *"Pan-Asian Cultural Diplomacy Symposium 2026"*)
   - Description
   - Date & Time
   - Venue (e.g., *"Kyoto International Conference Hall"*)
   - City & Country
   - Optional Registration / RSVP link
4. Click **Save & Publish Event**.
5. Your event now automatically appears on your public **Events** page and homepage!

---

## 7. How to Announce a Public Meetup

The Meetups section is specifically designed for your public appearances (campus visits, open coffee dialogues, cultural festivals).

1. Click **Manage Meetups** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Announce New Meetup**.
3. Fill in:
   - Title (e.g., *"University Visit & Open Dialogue"*)
   - Description
   - Date
   - Attendance Window (e.g., *"14:30 - 16:30 JST"*)
   - Venue & City
   - How to Participate (e.g., *"Free admission. Open to students and faculty."*)
   - Optional RSVP Link
4. Click **Announce Meetup**.

*Privacy Notice: Only announce appearances you explicitly choose to make public. You can change their status to "Completed" or remove them at any moment.*

---

## 8. How to Back Up Your Entire Website Data

You never have to worry about losing your essays or data:

1. Click **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Scroll to the **Full Database Backup** section.
3. Click the blue button: **Download Database Snapshot (.JSON)**.
4. A complete backup file containing all your articles, reader comments, events, meetups, and settings will download to your laptop. Keep this file in a safe place.
