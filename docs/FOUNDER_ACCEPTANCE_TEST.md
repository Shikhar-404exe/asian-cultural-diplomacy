# Founder Acceptance Testing & Mobile Walkthrough Guide

**Welcome to your Asian Cultural Diplomacy Platform.**

This guide is designed for you to test and verify your new website directly from your **mobile phone or tablet**—without touching a terminal, code, or developer tools. Every action can be completed using your phone's standard web browser (Safari, Chrome, or Firefox).

---

## What to Expect
- **Visual Design:** You will see your signature Deep Diplomatic Navy (`#142D4E`) and Warm Ivory (`#F5F1E8`) aesthetic, alongside original watercolor paintings (*The Bridge of Dialogue* and *The Courtyard of Contemplation*).
- **Public Discretion:** Visitors will see only your public essays, events, and an option to sign in. The administration portal is private and accessed directly via `/admin`.

---

## Helpful Tips for Publishing on Mobile

1. **Toolbar on Phone:** When editing text, the formatting toolbar stays fixed at the top of the editor. You can tap **B** for bold, **H2** for subheadings, or **Quote** for blockquotes.
2. **Uploading Photography from Your Phone Camera:**
   - Tap **Media & Artwork** in your menu.
   - Tap **Upload Photograph**.
   - Your phone will prompt you to choose from your Photo Library or take a photo with your camera.
   - The photo uploads securely and gives you a link to insert into your articles.
3. **Signing Out:** Tap your avatar or **Sign Out** at the bottom of your menu when you finish.
