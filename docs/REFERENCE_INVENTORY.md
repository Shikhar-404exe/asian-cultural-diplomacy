## Detailed Asset Documentation

### 1. `images.jpg` — Founder's Sister's Watercolor Moon Bridge
- **Visual Style**: Traditional ink wash and watercolor on textured mulberry/rice paper.
- **Color Profile**:
  - Ink navy / Charcoal black (`#142D4E`, `#1F2937`)
  - Glowing red-orange lanterns (`#E05A47`, `#F28E2B`)
  - Soft pink sakura blossom wash (`#F4D1D1`)
  - Warm parchment / ivory water reflections (`#F5F1E8`, `#E9E1D3`)
- **Symbolism**: The bridge connecting distant shores is the quintessential visual metaphor for Cultural Diplomacy. The paper lanterns represent illumination, mutual understanding, and warmth.
- **Application in MVP**:
  - Featured in the "Our Philosophy" / "Cultural Diplomacy" narrative sections.
  - Incorporated as a high-fidelity visual emblem on the About page and Homepage Philosophy banner.
  - Used in article illustrations exploring intercultural dialogue.

### 2. `images (1).jpg` — Classical Courtyard Pavilion & Peonies
- **Visual Style**: Traditional fine-brush gongbi architectural painting.
- **Color Profile**:
  - Imperial jade / emerald green tiles (`#2D7F67`, `#1A5340`)
  - Vermilion lacquer pillars and floral blooms (`#C84B31`, `#D9534F`)
  - Bamboo greens and pine needles (`#4A6B53`)
  - Soft cream wall surfaces (`#E9E1D3`, `#F5F1E8`)
- **Symbolism**: Architectural integrity, cultural heritage, flourishing dialogue (blooming peonies), resilience (bamboo and pine).
- **Application in MVP**:
  - Hero cultural perspective cards on Homepage.
  - Background imagery for cultural publications and editorial category cards.
  - Media asset for Founder's bio / About Section.
